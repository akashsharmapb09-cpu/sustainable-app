YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

GreenSwap helps you explore practical sustainable-lifestyle alternatives. This page explains what information this website handles and the choices available to you.

1. Information you enter

You may choose preferences such as travel habits, diet, electricity use, food-waste habits, budget and priorities. These answers are used in your browser to display recommendations for the current session.

2. Local processing and storage

The current GreenSwap experience is designed to process preference selections and shortlist interactions in your browser. The site does not currently require you to create an account or submit these preferences to a GreenSwap server. Some browser features or hosting services may retain standard technical data as described in their own policies.

Unless a feature explicitly tells you otherwise, do not enter sensitive personal information into the site.

3. Cookies and analytics

GreenSwap's current interface does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run a dedicated analytics tracker. The hosting provider, content delivery network, or embedded third-party resources may process limited technical information needed to deliver the site. Google Fonts is loaded from Google servers, which may receive standard connection data when fonts are requested.

4. Third-party links

The methodology page links to external research and public sources. When you follow an external link, that destination's privacy policy and practices apply. GreenSwap does not control third-party websites.

5. Why information is used

Preference choices are used to tailor sustainable-lifestyle suggestions, show relevant alternatives, and support the interactive 7-day action plan. Recommendations are educational suggestions, not guarantees of environmental savings.

6. Sharing and selling

GreenSwap does not sell the preference details you enter in its current browser-based recommendation flow. We do not intentionally transmit those selections to third parties. Technical data may be processed by the infrastructure and resources required to host and display the website.

7. Children

GreenSwap is a general-audience informational website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit personal or sensitive information through the site.

8. Security and retention

Browser-based interactions generally remain in the active page session. We cannot guarantee the security of your device, browser, network, or third-party hosting infrastructure. Clear your browser session or close the page if you want to end the current interaction.

9. Your choices

You can use the site without entering optional preferences, and you can change selections before generating recommendations. You can leave the website at any time. Browser settings can also control third-party resource requests, although some visual features may not display as intended.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if GreenSwap's features or data practices change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

11. Contact

If you have a question about this policy, contact the website owner through the GitHub repository: GreenSwap project support ↗.

Transparency note: this policy describes the current browser-based demo as implemented today. If accounts, analytics, forms, or server-side preference storage are added later, update this page to reflect those changes before launch.
